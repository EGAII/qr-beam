Send to your phone

Text or a file becomes an animated QR code. The phone reads it with its camera. Nothing is uploaded.

Open the receiver on your phone

  1. Scan this code with the phone's camera app to open the receiver.
  2. On the phone, tap Start camera.
  3. Here, enter text or choose a file, then press Start sending.
  4. Hold the phone so the code fills most of its view until it says it's done.
